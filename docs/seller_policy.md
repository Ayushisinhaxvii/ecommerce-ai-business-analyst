# Mercato — Seller Policy

## Seller Responsibilities

Mercato sellers are responsible for maintaining accurate product information and fulfilling customer orders according to marketplace requirements.

Seller responsibilities include:

- Maintaining accurate product listings.
- Keeping product availability information updated.
- Preparing orders within the required fulfillment timeframe.
- Providing the correct products to customers.
- Packaging products appropriately.
- Cooperating with delivery and return processes.

## Product Listings

Sellers should provide accurate information about:

- Product name
- Product description
- Product category
- Product availability
- Product specifications
- Product images

Incorrect or incomplete product information can negatively affect customer expectations and satisfaction.

## Order Fulfillment

Sellers should process orders promptly after receiving them.

Repeated fulfillment delays may negatively affect:

- Delivery performance
- Customer satisfaction
- Seller performance
- Marketplace reliability

## Seller Performance Monitoring

Mercato evaluates seller performance using indicators such as:

- Sales volume
- Number of orders
- Order fulfillment performance
- Delivery performance
- Customer review scores
- Cancellation and order failure rates

## Seller Support

Mercato should use seller-level analytics to identify sellers who consistently perform well and sellers who may require operational support.

High-performing sellers can provide useful examples of effective fulfillment and customer service practices.

## Operational Improvement

When a seller shows repeated operational problems, Mercato should investigate the underlying cause before determining an appropriate corrective action.

Potential causes may include:

- Inventory management issues
- Fulfillment delays
- Product availability problems
- Packaging issues
- Logistics constraints
- Inaccurate product information
