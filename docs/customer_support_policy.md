# Mercato — Customer Support Policy

## Customer Support Goals

Mercato aims to provide timely and helpful support throughout the customer order lifecycle.

Customer support should help customers resolve issues related to:

- Orders
- Payments
- Deliveries
- Product problems
- Returns
- Refunds
- Cancellations

## Order Support

Customers should be able to request assistance when they experience problems with an order.

Common order-related issues include:

- Delayed delivery
- Incorrect products
- Damaged products
- Missing products
- Order cancellation
- Product availability issues

## Delivery Support

When an order is significantly delayed, customers should receive clear information about the expected delivery status.

Delivery-related complaints should be analyzed together with seller and logistics performance to identify recurring problems.

## Refund and Return Support

Customer support should explain the applicable return and refund process and help customers understand the status of their requests.

Refund requests should be handled according to Mercato's refund and cancellation policy.

## Customer Satisfaction

Mercato monitors customer reviews and feedback to identify areas where the customer experience can be improved.

Low review scores may indicate problems related to:

- Delivery
- Product quality
- Seller performance
- Customer support
- Order fulfillment

## Business Analytics

Customer support data should be combined with business analytics whenever possible.

For example, Mercato can investigate whether customers with delayed deliveries are more likely to provide lower review scores.

The AI Business Analyst can combine operational data with company policies to help identify potential causes and recommend areas for investigation.
