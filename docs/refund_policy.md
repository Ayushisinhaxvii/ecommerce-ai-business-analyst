# Mercato — Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of an order before the order has been shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be available and the customer may need to follow the return process instead.

## Eligible Refunds

Customers may be eligible for a refund when:

- An order is canceled before shipment.
- A product is damaged or defective.
- The wrong product was delivered.
- An order cannot be fulfilled because the product is unavailable.
- A return is approved according to Mercato's return conditions.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are initiated after the cancellation or return request has been reviewed.

The time required for the refund to reach the customer's original payment method may depend on the payment provider.

## Non-Eligible Situations

A refund may be rejected when:

- The request does not satisfy Mercato's return or refund conditions.
- The product has been used or damaged by the customer beyond normal handling.
- The customer submits the request outside the applicable return period.

## Business Considerations

Mercato monitors cancellations and refunds to identify recurring operational problems.

A high number of cancellations or refund requests for a particular seller, product, or category may indicate issues with:

- Inventory availability
- Product quality
- Product descriptions
- Order fulfillment
- Seller performance

Analytics should be used together with refund and cancellation information to identify areas requiring investigation.
