# Business Insights

## 1. Sales Performance

### Finding
The analysis shows a strong overall increase in product sales over the observation period, particularly throughout 2017. Sales continued at a high level during 2018, although month-to-month fluctuations were visible.

### Business Interpretation
The marketplace experienced substantial growth in sales activity over time. However, the fluctuations in monthly sales suggest that sales performance is not completely stable and may be influenced by seasonal or operational factors.

### Business Recommendation
The business should investigate the months with unusually high and low sales to identify seasonal patterns, promotional effects, and operational factors that influence sales performance.

## 2. Customer Retention

### Finding
The customer base is heavily dominated by one-time purchasers. Most unique customers placed only one order, while a much smaller portion returned for additional purchases.

The repeat customer rate is approximately 3.12%.

### Business Interpretation
The marketplace has a strong customer acquisition base, but relatively low repeat purchasing. This indicates that customer retention is an important business opportunity.

### Business Recommendation
The business should focus on retention strategies such as personalized product recommendations, post-purchase engagement, loyalty programs, and targeted offers designed to encourage customers to make a second purchase.

## 3. Delivery Performance

### Finding
The analysis shows that average delivery time varies across different months. Most months show delivery times around the 10–15 day range, while some earlier periods have noticeably higher delivery times.

### Business Interpretation
Delivery performance is not completely consistent across the entire observation period. Periods with unusually high delivery times may indicate operational or logistics challenges.

### Business Recommendation
The business should investigate months with unusually long delivery times and identify whether the issue is related to sellers, logistics capacity, geographic regions, or order volume.

## 4. Delivery Time and Customer Satisfaction

### Finding
The analysis shows a negative relationship between delivery time and customer review score. Customers giving lower ratings generally experienced longer delivery times.

The correlation between delivery time and review score was approximately -0.334.

### Business Interpretation
Longer delivery times are associated with lower customer satisfaction. However, correlation does not prove that delivery delays directly cause lower ratings.

### Business Recommendation
The business should prioritize reducing delivery delays and investigate sellers, regions, and logistics processes associated with longer delivery times. Improving delivery reliability could help improve the overall customer experience.

## 5. Product Category Performance

### Finding
The analysis identifies clear differences in sales contribution across product categories. A relatively small group of categories contributes a significant portion of overall product sales.

### Business Interpretation
Product demand is not evenly distributed across the marketplace. High-performing categories represent important revenue drivers, while lower-performing categories may require further investigation.

### Business Recommendation
The business should prioritize high-performing categories through inventory planning, seller acquisition, and targeted promotions while investigating whether lower-performing categories have pricing, demand, or assortment issues.

## 6. Seller Performance

### Finding
The analysis shows that sales are distributed across a large number of sellers. The top 10 sellers contribute approximately 13.27% of total product sales.

### Business Interpretation
The marketplace has a relatively diversified seller ecosystem rather than depending heavily on a small number of sellers. However, individual seller performance can still vary in terms of sales and delivery performance.

### Business Recommendation
The business should identify high-performing sellers and understand the practices that contribute to their success. At the same time, sellers with weaker delivery performance should be investigated to identify opportunities for improving logistics and customer experience.

## 7. Payment Behavior

### Finding
The analysis shows that payment activity is distributed across multiple payment methods, with some methods contributing substantially more payment value than others.

### Business Interpretation
Customers have different payment preferences, and the distribution of payment value across methods provides insight into how customers prefer to complete purchases.

### Business Recommendation
The business should continue supporting the major payment methods used by customers while monitoring changes in payment preferences. Payment methods with strong adoption should be prioritized for a smooth and reliable checkout experience.

## 8. Order Status and Cancellation Performance

### Finding
The analysis shows that the majority of orders were successfully delivered, while a smaller portion of orders were canceled, unavailable, shipped, invoiced, or remained in other processing stages.

The cancellation rate is approximately 0.63%.

### Business Interpretation
The high proportion of delivered orders indicates that most orders successfully reach customers. However, canceled and unavailable orders represent potential lost sales and customer experience issues.

### Business Recommendation
The business should monitor canceled and unavailable orders and investigate their underlying causes. The analysis should consider factors such as seller availability, inventory issues, payment problems, and operational constraints to reduce preventable order failures.
