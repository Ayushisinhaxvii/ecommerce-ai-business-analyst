# Mercato — Delivery and Shipping Policy

## Delivery Estimates

Mercato provides customers with an estimated delivery date when an order is placed.

The estimated delivery date is based on the expected fulfillment and shipping process.

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and may be affected by operational or logistical circumstances.

## Seller Fulfillment

Sellers are responsible for preparing products for shipment within the required fulfillment timeframe.

Delays in seller fulfillment can affect the overall delivery time experienced by the customer.

## Delivery Delays

Delivery delays may occur because of:

- Seller fulfillment delays
- Product availability issues
- Logistics capacity
- Transportation disruptions
- Geographic factors
- Unexpected operational issues

Mercato should monitor delivery performance to identify recurring sources of delays.

## Late Deliveries

An order is considered late when the customer receives the order after the estimated delivery date.

Mercato tracks late delivery performance using the late delivery rate.

## Customer Experience

Customers should receive accurate delivery estimates and timely updates about their orders.

When significant delays occur, customer communication should be prioritized to reduce uncertainty and improve the overall customer experience.

## Business Monitoring

Mercato should analyze delivery performance by:

- Month
- Seller
- Product category
- Customer region
- Order volume

This analysis can help identify operational bottlenecks and opportunities to improve delivery reliability.

## Delivery and Customer Satisfaction

Mercato recognizes that delivery performance can influence customer satisfaction.

Longer delivery times should therefore be investigated alongside customer review scores to identify potential relationships between operational performance and customer experience.
