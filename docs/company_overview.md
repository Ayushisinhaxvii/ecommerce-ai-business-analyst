# Mercato — Company Overview

## Company Description

Mercato is an online e-commerce marketplace that connects customers with independent sellers across multiple product categories.

The platform manages customer orders, payments, deliveries, product listings, seller relationships, and customer reviews.

## Business Model

Mercato operates as a multi-seller marketplace.

Customers purchase products through the Mercato platform, while independent sellers are responsible for listing products and fulfilling orders.

Mercato's business performance is evaluated using metrics such as:

- Sales
- Order volume
- Average Order Value
- Customer retention
- Delivery performance
- Customer satisfaction
- Seller performance

## Business Priorities

Mercato's primary business priorities are:

1. Increase sales and order volume.
2. Improve customer retention and repeat purchases.
3. Improve delivery reliability.
4. Maintain high customer satisfaction.
5. Support high-performing sellers.
6. Reduce preventable order cancellations.
7. Improve the overall customer shopping experience.

## Customer Experience

Mercato aims to provide customers with a reliable shopping experience from product discovery through order delivery.

Important aspects of the customer experience include:

- Product availability
- Competitive pricing
- Reliable payment processing
- Accurate delivery estimates
- Timely delivery
- Responsive customer support
- Simple return and refund processes

## Seller Ecosystem

Mercato works with independent sellers who list products on the marketplace.

Seller performance can vary based on:

- Sales volume
- Product availability
- Order fulfillment
- Delivery performance
- Customer reviews

Mercato should use seller-level analytics to identify strong performers and support sellers who require operational improvement.

## Data and Analytics

Mercato uses business analytics to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product demand, seller performance, delivery operations, and customer satisfaction.

The AI Business Analyst system combines structured business data with company knowledge documents to answer business questions and support decision-making.
